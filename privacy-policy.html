Vandana Karthikeya AI Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Overview

Vandana Karthikeya AI Lab ("the Site") hosts browser-based portfolio prototypes and learning tools developed by VK. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how these tools work.

Microphone Access

Some learning tools may request access to your device's microphone solely for browser-based audio practice or pitch detection. Audio from the microphone is processed on your device using browser APIs. No audio is recorded, stored, saved to files, or transmitted to any server by these static site prototypes.

Data Collection

The Site does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. Specifically:

We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying information. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not transmit any data over the internet. The App functions entirely offline.

Local Storage

Some tools may store demo preferences, votes, feedback drafts, practice scores, or session history locally on your device using browser local storage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared through your browser settings or through any in-app clear/reset option where available.

Third-Party Services

The Site may load external fonts, browser libraries, or links to third-party sites such as LinkedIn and PayPal. Portfolio prototypes are static demos and do not send patient, claims, audio, or personal health data to a backend service.

Children's Privacy

The Site does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
